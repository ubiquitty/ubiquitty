# Built-in theme credits

`builtin-themes.toml` ships color schemes adapted from the upstream projects
below. A color scheme is a small set of palette values; each family is credited
to its source and is used under the listed license. "Noir" and its light
counterpart "Noir Clair" are original to Ubiquitty. "ANSI Light" / "ANSI Dark"
are generic standard 16-color ANSI palettes (no third-party attribution).

The [full upstream notices](../../../Docs/third-party-licenses/Theme-LICENSES.txt)
include the license texts and their source identities. The combined palette
file retains Apache-2.0 for Ubiquitty's original material and the applicable
MIT/Apache-2.0 terms for the adapted palettes.
