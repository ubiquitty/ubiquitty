# The iOS driver (Source/Platform/iOS/App/IOSSnapshotLaunch.swift, through
# Scripts/ios-snapshot-shim.sh) renders every case the catalog holds; an entry
# here is a renderer gap, named in its reason.

# A line may carry a pixel ceiling — `identifier  [max Npx] why (date)` — which
# reds when the difference grows past it; see known-failing-macos.txt for the
# rule.

# smooths no glyph on iOS, and a rule's weight is the face's own underscore as
# iOS draws it), is held to its own picture in golden/native/coretext-ios
# rather than listed here.
